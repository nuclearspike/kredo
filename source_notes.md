# Source Notes and External Anchors

These notes anchor the demo to current public Kiva concepts while keeping this as a reimagined proposal, not a clone.

## Public Kiva concept anchors used

- Kiva describes itself as an international nonprofit that connects people through crowdfunded loans and addresses financial exclusion.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/about

- Kiva says Lending Partners include microfinance institutions, nonprofits, and other organizations that disburse loans in served communities; Kiva says it does not take fees from lenders or borrowers and individual lenders do not earn interest.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/about/how

- Kiva explains partner-facilitated loans, pre-disbursal, monthly partner funding, and that repayments are passed back to lenders.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/blog/how-kiva-works-with-lending-partners

- Kiva developer docs expose GraphQL API methods and a schema explorer/docs flow.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/build/getting-started

- Kiva review/translation volunteer pages describe volunteers translating loan profiles and reviewing stories for accuracy.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/work-with-us/reviewers

- Kiva due diligence pages distinguish partner-facilitated loans and direct loans, with direct loans common in the U.S.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/about/due-diligence

- Kiva direct loan due diligence describes internal checks such as identity verification, financial history review, watchlist screening, business viability, potential social impact, and social underwriting.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/about/due-diligence/direct-loans

- Kiva social underwriting content describes trustees/hubs/community partners providing endorsements and wraparound support.
  Source: https://www.kiva.org/blog/what-is-social-underwriting

## Methodology anchors used

- Living Systems Software Development: outcome-first, subsystem-first, reveal/dialogue/UI layering, command-safe AI.
- StoryTime Surface Projection: actor stages, artifact/detail stories, decision sets, reveal edges, command registry responsibility.
- FractalFlow DSL: canonical graph/AST, six dialects, evidence labels, non-Turing declarative core, questions instead of guessed authority/lifetime/visibility/policy.
