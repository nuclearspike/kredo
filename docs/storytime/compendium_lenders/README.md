# Lender Compendium

A complete enumeration of **every kind of lender** in Kredo and every journey
they live — the idea-generating mode of LSSD rather than the audit mode. Where
the audits in the parent directory ask *"what is broken for this person?"*, a
compendium asks *"who are all the people, and what is every situation they can
possibly be in?"*

It found **14 distinct lender types**, several of which existed in the running
product and in nobody's mental model.

Read in order; each file is a pass of the method.

| File | Pass | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| [`00_scope_and_cast.md`](00_scope_and_cast.md) | Cast | The 14-plane sweep. Every category of actor either named or ruled out **in writing** — two of the rule-outs are themselves findings |
| [`01_ladders.md`](01_ladders.md) | Ladders | 67 stages across seven life bands per actor, from first contact to leaving |
| [`02_stage_records.md`](02_stage_records.md) | Stage records | 18 stages in full: what the person can see and do, and their **interior monologue and ranked questions** in their own voice |
| [`03_walks_and_storm.md`](03_walks_and_storm.md) | Walks + storm | 28 consequence walks, then all 14 standard failure families run against the plane |
| [`04_reveals_artifacts_details.md`](04_reveals_artifacts_details.md) | Reveals + artifacts | Who gets told what, the communication catalog, and the lifecycle of everything the product stores |
| [`05_matrices_counts_gates.md`](05_matrices_counts_gates.md) | Close-out | Matrices, the count block, the gate report, and **19 open product questions** |

**Two gates are declared PARTIAL rather than claimed**: 18 of 67 stages carry
full records, and the completeness critic never reached two dry rounds before the
methodology's bounded stop at four. It ran in fresh context and produced 27 hits
across those four rounds, all folded back in — including whole actor types the
first pass missed.

Two claims raised here were **withdrawn against evidence** rather than quietly
deleted; both corrections are left in the text.
